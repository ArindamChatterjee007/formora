High-Protein Foods: The Best Sources

Protein keeps you full, protects muscle when you're losing fat, and builds it when you're growing. Here are the best sources — veg, vegan and non-veg — with rough protein per serving.

How much do you need? Aim for 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg of bodyweight per day. For a 70 kg person that's ~112–154 g. Spread it across 3–5 meals — your body uses it best that way.

Simple ways to hit your target

  • Anchor every meal around a protein source first, then add carbs and veg.
  • Keep a fast option handy — eggs, curd, whey or soya — for when you're short.
  • Vegan? Soya chunks, tofu, lentils and a plant protein powder make it easy.

FAQ

Is too much protein bad?

For healthy people, high-protein diets are safe. Stay hydrated and, if you have kidney issues, follow your doctor's advice.

Do I need protein powder?

No — it's just a convenient, cheap way to top up. Whole foods can cover your needs entirely.
