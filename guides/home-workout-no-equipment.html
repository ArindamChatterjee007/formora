Home Workout, No Equipment: Start Today

You don't need a gym, weights or any gear to build muscle and get lean — you need your bodyweight, a bit of floor space and consistency. Here's a full plan.

The 6 exercises that cover your whole body

  • Push-ups — chest, shoulders, triceps. (Easier: on knees or against a wall.)
  • Squats — quads, glutes, legs. (Harder: slow tempo or jump squats.)
  • Lunges — legs + balance, one side at a time.
  • Glute bridges — glutes + lower back.
  • Plank — the whole core, no crunching.
  • Superman / back extensions — the back muscles push-ups miss.

Your weekly plan (3 days, ~25 minutes)

Do this circuit 3 times per week (e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri). Rest a day between. Complete all exercises, rest 60–90s, then repeat for 3 rounds.

How to keep getting stronger without weights

The secret is progressive overload — making it harder over time. Without weights you do that by:

  • Adding reps or rounds each week
  • Slowing the tempo (3 seconds down on every rep)
  • Moving to harder versions (knee → full → decline push-ups)
  • Shortening rest between exercises
Consistency beats intensity. Three focused 25-minute sessions a week, done every week for 3 months, will change your body more than one brutal workout you never repeat.

FAQ

Can you build muscle with just bodyweight?

Yes — especially as a beginner. Once bodyweight gets easy, harder variations (one-arm, elevated, slow-tempo) keep the challenge going for a long time.

How long until I see results?

Strength improves in 2–4 weeks; visible changes in 6–12 weeks with consistent training + reasonable eating.
