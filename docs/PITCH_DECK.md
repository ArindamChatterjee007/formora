### Slide 2 — Problem
- **80%+ of people quit fitness apps** — solo trackers are lonely and generic.
- Two broken options today: **dumb loggers** (MyFitnessPal) or **expensive human coaches**.
- What people actually want: **a specific look, a plan that adapts, and accountability.**

> Speaker note: retention in fitness apps averages **<30% at year one** (RevenueCat) — the industry's core failure is keeping people engaged.

---

### Slide 3 — Solution
**Formora = Goal + AI Coach + Community, in one app.**
- Pick the **look** you want → adaptive workouts + AI meal plans toward it.
- **Social feed** (Flex reels, crew, challenges) = accountability + motivation.
- Instagram-grade **camera** turns progress into shareable content.

---

### Slide 4 — Product (show, don't tell)
Screenshots: onboarding → physique goal → adaptive plan → feed/Flex → progress charts.
**Already live** (web + installable on iOS/Android) at `formora`. Real, not a mockup.

### Slide 5 — Why now
- Fitness-app revenue **$3.4B in 2025, +24.5% YoY**; **540M users** (Business of Apps).
- **AI** makes personalized coaching cheap at scale (our premium hook).
- **Creator economy**: fitness influencers need tools to monetize an audience — we give them one.

---

### Slide 6 — Market
- **TAM:** global fitness-app revenue **~$3.4B**, double-digit growth.
- **SAM:** physique/strength + social-fitness (English + India) — the fastest-growing, highest-intent slice.
- **SOM (3-yr):** capture a low-single-digit-% niche → **$0.2–1M+ ARR** path, starting India-first.

---

### Slide 7 — Business model
**Freemium subscription + hybrid.**
- **Pro $7.99/mo · $49.99/yr**, **Elite $19.99/mo** (PPP for India).
- **Web-first billing → keep the 30% Apple/Google would take** (structural margin edge).
- Plus **affiliate**, **coaching marketplace (15–20% take)**, **creator programs**, **B2B white-label**.
- We're in the **highest-LTV category in mobile** (H&F leads realized LTV; ~44% trial→paid).

---

### Slide 8 — Go-to-market (near-zero CAC)
- **Viral content loop:** every shared Flex reel is watermarked → free TikTok/IG/Shorts reach.
- **Community-led retention:** crews + challenges + streaks fight the industry's churn problem.
- **Referral + micro-influencer** seeding; **India-first** (home advantage, huge market), premium in NA/EU.

---

### Slide 9 — Competition
2×2: **Solo ↔ Social** (x) × **Generic ↔ Aesthetic-goal** (y).
- MyFitnessPal/Fitbod/Hevy → solo + generic.
- Strava → social but cardio.
- **Formora → social + aesthetic-goal (top-right, empty quadrant).**
Moat = **community network effect** (switching cost + free virality) that solo apps can't copy.

---

### Slide 10 — Traction (honest, early)
- **Live product** across web + Android + iOS builds; **PWA** (free install, no store fee).
- **CI/CD release pipeline** (dev→release→beta→main) + **ISO-aligned security** (CSP, PBKDF2, RLS in progress) — engineering maturity rare at this stage.
- **Early users + real social feed** seeded; **pre-revenue** — monetization layer is the next milestone (spec'd, ~2 weeks out).

> Speaker note: don't inflate. The story is "de-risked product + clear path to first revenue," not fake MRR.

- Be blunt about being pre-revenue + solo; pair it with the **spec'd, weeks-away** monetization plan. Investors fund *momentum + clarity*, and honesty builds trust.
